Pixel Lab

An image is a grid of tiny squares. Paint some, then see the numbers the computer keeps for them.

Click or drag across the squares to paint with the colour on the right.

Colour

#1E88F0 brush colour

Black and white

Each square keeps only one bit: 0 = black, 1 = white. Click a square to flip it.

Squares brighter than this become 1, the rest become 0.
